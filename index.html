    How it works

    Three steps to a focused day.

    1. 1

      Connect your lists

      Sign in to iCloud and Google once. Today's reminders, events and tasks appear in the widget.

    2. 2

      Pick what matters now

      Choose one task from today's list and set how long you want to work on it.

    3. 3

      Focus, then check it off

      Start the timer, let your chosen apps go quiet, and tick the task when the session ends.

    Questions

    Where do my tasks come from?

    From iCloud Reminders, Google Calendar and Google Tasks. DeskFocus shows what's due today from all three in one list.

    What happens to notifications during focus?

    Notifications from the apps you selected are held back while a timer runs. Everything else works as usual.

    Is it on the Mac App Store?

    No — DeskFocus is a direct download from this site.

    Which Macs does it run on?

    Any Mac running [MACOS VERSION] or later.
