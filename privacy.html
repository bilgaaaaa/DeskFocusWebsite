Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to Deskfocus for Mac, Deskfocus for iPhone and iPad, and this website.

The short version

  • Your tasks, events and focus sessions stay on your devices. Deskfocus has no servers of its own and no user accounts.
  • Google data travels only between your device and Google. It is never sold, used for ads, or used to train AI models.
  • There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking in the apps.
  • If you buy Deskfocus Pro, Apple or Paddle handles the payment. Deskfocus never sees your card details.

1. Who is responsible

Deskfocus is made by [DEVELOPER NAME], an independent developer based in Padova, Italy, who is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For any privacy question or request, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. Data that stays on your device

Deskfocus works locally. The following is read or stored on your Mac, iPhone or iPad and is never sent to the developer:

  • Apple Calendar and Reminders. With your permission, Deskfocus reads today's events and reminders to build your list, creates an event or reminder when you add a task, and marks a reminder done when you finish it. Apple syncs these through iCloud according to your own iCloud settings.
  • Focus sessions and settings. Your timer, chosen apps, visible lists, colours and other preferences are saved in a file shared only between the Deskfocus app and its widget on the same device.
  • Apps you choose to silence (Mac). To let you pick apps, Deskfocus looks at the apps installed and running on your Mac. During a session it hides or quits the apps you selected and shows or reopens them afterwards. This list never leaves your Mac.
  • Focus mode Shortcut (Mac). If you set one, Deskfocus runs the Shortcut you named to switch a macOS Focus mode. It does not read your other Shortcuts.
  • Notifications and Live Activities. "Time is up" alerts and the Lock Screen timer are created on your device. Deskfocus does not use push notification servers.
  • Diagnostic logs. The apps write technical logs to Apple's on-device logging system. They stay on your device unless you choose to share them, for example when reporting a bug.

3. Google Calendar and Google Tasks

Connecting a Google account is optional. If you do, Deskfocus asks Google for these permissions and uses them only for the features below:

PermissionWhat Deskfocus does with it
Your email addressShows which Google account is connected in Settings and in the "Add task" menu.
See your Google Calendar (read-only)Lists your calendars so you can choose which to show, and shows their events in your day and week view. Deskfocus never changes or deletes calendar events.
Create and edit your Google TasksShows your task lists and tasks, adds a new task when you create one in Deskfocus, and marks a task completed when you finish it. Deskfocus does not delete tasks.

All requests go directly from your device to Google's servers. Google's sign-in tokens are stored in your device's Keychain. Your Google data is kept only in your device's memory and in the app's local file for display, and is refreshed from Google each time. When you disconnect Google in Settings, Deskfocus deletes the tokens from the Keychain and asks Google to revoke its access. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deskfocus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, data received from Google APIs:

  • is used only to provide the features described above, which you can see in the app;
  • is not transferred to anyone, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your notice;
  • is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to determine creditworthiness;
  • is never used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models;
  • is not read by any person, unless you give your explicit permission for specific items (for example, when asking for support), it is needed for security purposes, or it is required by law.

4. Purchases and Deskfocus Pro

Deskfocus Pro unlocks extra features on all your devices. Payments are handled by:

  • Apple, for purchases made in the iPhone or iPad app through the App Store, under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Paddle, for purchases of the Mac app on this website. Paddle is the merchant of record: it processes your payment, handles tax and sends your receipt, under Paddle's Privacy Policy.

To keep Pro unlocked on every device, Deskfocus uses RevenueCat to record which purchases belong to you. RevenueCat receives a random app user identifier, the product you bought, purchase and renewal dates, your country and the receipt from Apple or Paddle, under RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. If you use Sign in with Apple to restore Pro on another device, Deskfocus receives only Apple's anonymous user identifier for this app, which links your devices to the same purchase. Deskfocus never receives your card or bank details.

The developer receives sales reports from Apple and Paddle, which may include your country, the product, the price and, for Paddle purchases, the email address and name you gave at checkout. These are used only for support, refunds and tax records.

5. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. When you visit it, your browser connects to:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site and may log your IP address for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement;
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typeface and receives your IP address and browser details, under the Google Privacy Policy;
  • the GitHub API, which provides the star and download counts shown on the home page.

If you email the developer, your email and its contents are used only to reply to you.

6. Legal basis and retention

  • Providing the features you ask for (showing your lists, Google sync, Pro on all your devices) is based on performing our agreement with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Access to your calendars, reminders and Google account also depends on the permission you give, which you can withdraw at any time in system Settings or in Deskfocus.
  • Purchase and tax records are kept for as long as Italian tax and accounting law requires, which is currently ten years (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Website security logs and support emails are based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). Support emails are deleted once your request is resolved, unless they relate to a purchase.

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall Deskfocus.

7. International transfers

Apple, Google, Paddle, RevenueCat and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. They do so under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of the personal data the developer holds about you, and object to its use, by writing to [CONTACT EMAIL]. You will get an answer within one month. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or to the authority in your own country.

9. Children

Deskfocus is not directed at children under 14, or under the minimum age for digital consent in your country, and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes, such as a new type of data or a new service provider, will also be announced in the app's release notes before they take effect.
